## Reference sites — the niche's best (all real, live sites)

### 1. Drybar — https://www.drybar.com
**What makes it work:** one signature colour (bright yellow) carried through
every page, button and headline — the brand is recognisable in one scroll.
Playful, energetic personality; crisp product/service images; booking, shop
and locations reachable without hunting. Short service descriptions, clear
next step in seconds.
**Takeaway for Gal:** pick ONE unmissable signature colour and own it
everywhere (our pick: deep vinho/burgundy + gold, see §3). Booking CTA
visible on every screen.

### 2. Sassoon Salon — https://www.sassoon.com
**What makes it work:** luxury through restraint — minimalist black-and-white,
high-definition hair photography, generous white space, few words, few fonts.
Heritage → stylist portfolio in smooth scrolling transitions. Education
content positions the brand as the expert; booking buttons sit exactly where
a ready client looks.
**Takeaway for Gal:** let the best photos do the selling; editorial restraint
on the gallery pages; short, confident copy; stylist/artist profile as a
trust anchor (Gal herself is the product).

### 3. Tiara Salon (Lake Forest, IL) — https://tiarasalon.com
**What makes it work:** imagery leads — full-screen split-screen image
scroller, black-and-white portraits sliding apart vertically on scroll;
modern without being busy. Clean hamburger menu keeps focus on photos, not
text. Shows the RANGE of clients served.
**Takeaway for Gal:** full-bleed, image-led hero and gallery; show the real
range — noivas, formandas, madrinhas, pele madura, pele negra, cachos —
representation builds trust faster than a tagline. (Use her real IG photos
where available; otherwise clearly-labelled style placeholders — never
invented client photos presented as real.)

### 4. Album Hair (Toronto) — https://www.albumhair.com
**What makes it work:** hero video of the salon + immediate booking option;
a sticky "Book a Last Minute Appointment" button that follows the visitor
down the page. Closes the gap between interest and action — the friction-free
flow.
**Takeaway for Gal:** a sticky "Agendar no WhatsApp" button on every page,
especially mobile; the booking-bot mock page IS the friction-killer (client
picks occasion → date → service → confirms in the chat mock, then jumps to
real WhatsApp). 80% of salon guests want to book on mobile (Zenoti 2025).

### 5. Curl Kitchen Salon (Chicago) — https://curlkitchensalon.com
**What makes it work:** niche announced loudly in the first glance —
naturally curly hair, playful palette against real photos of the team's work;
a hair quiz matches visitors to the right service. Specialists win trust
fast: name the niche in the headline, prove it in the gallery.
**Takeaway for Gal:** name the niche in the hero headline — noivas e
formandas em Salvador — and make the bot mock a mini-quiz ("Qual é o seu
momento? Noiva / Madrinha / Formanda / Debutante") that routes to the right
package. Playful palette + real work photos.

## Design language chosen for this build — "Vinho Editorial" (FRESH — NOT the house template)

The house ivory/espresso template is BANNED here. This build is a bold, warm,
editorial bridal-glam language decided by the niche refs above:

- **Palette:** deep vinho (wine/burgundy, near #4A1026 / #5C1A30) as the dominant
  field — dark, dramatic, warm; **gold** (#C9A227 / #D4AF37) as the single
  metallic accent; blush/rose (#F6E3E0) as the light counterpoint; near-black
  plum for text on light. NO ivory paper background, NO espresso ink default.
- **Type:** oversized editorial display serif (Fraunces or Playfair Display)
  for headlines — huge, tight, uppercase kickers; a clean grotesque
  (Archivo / Inter) for UI and body. One script accent (e.g. italic serif)
  for the "realçar, não esconder" line only.
- **Layout idiom:** editorial magazine — full-bleed image panels, split-screen
  scroll moments (Tiara), oversized numerals for the 3-step booking flow,
  marquee strip with occasions (NOIVAS • MADRINHAS • FORMANDAS • DEBUTANTES).
- **Motion personality:** slow, confident, cinematic — hero image slow zoom,
  headline words rising in stagger, gold underline draw; the bot mock types
  and replies with a soft bounce. One hero move per moment; payoff stillness.
- **Photography rule:** her real work photos (from @realcebygal / the listing)
  lead; where a photo is unavailable, use a clearly-labelled style placeholder
  or a warm colour/texture panel — NEVER a fake client photo, NEVER a fake
  testimonial, NEVER stock faces presented as her clients.
- **Mobile-first:** sticky "Agendar no WhatsApp" button always visible;
  80% of salon guests book on mobile (Zenoti 2025 benchmark).
